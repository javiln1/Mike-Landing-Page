# RSA Proof Bank

Raw material for the new Mike ads (audience: people already in 9-5 sales jobs). Built 2026-10-27 from 22 videos. Every transcript is in `Sources/`.

**How to read the quotes:** they are machine transcripts (whisper large-v3-turbo) and haven't been hand-checked. Words are kept exactly as transcribed. `[...]` marks my fix for an obvious mishearing, e.g. "i-ticket" → "[high-]ticket". Timestamps are `mm:ss` in that video. Re-listen before putting a quote on screen.

**Biggest flags, up front:**

- **The live VSL attacks the new audience.** It says experienced salespeople are "the worst people I've ever trained" (VSL 02:43). See `VSL Comparison.md`.
- **No video mentions Dubai.** The VSL says Mike's company contacts are "across the US, the UK and Canada" (VSL 06:16).
- **Numbers conflict for almost every student** across the testimonial, the ads and the landing page. See section 3.
- **Zane came from a sales job** in his own words. The VSL says he "never sold anything in his life".
- **The landing page's three hero quotes** (Zain $12K, Kai $9.5K, Sam $15K) don't appear in any video. They may be placeholders.
