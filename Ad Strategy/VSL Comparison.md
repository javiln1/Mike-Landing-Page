## 3. What every new ad must match (or the VSL must change)

1. **The experience clash is the #1 risk.** A car-sales or corporate-sales viewer clicks an ad that flatters their experience, then hears Mike say people with "10 plus years of experience" are "the worst people I've ever trained … they are so bad" (02:43-03:03 and 08:28-08:47). Fix it one of two ways:
   - **Re-cut the VSL:** trim or reframe those two passages.
   - **Frame the ads to match the VSL:** experience is an advantage *only if you're coachable*. The VSL's own escape hatch is the ego, not the experience: "they come in with all this ego, think they don't need training".
2. **Zane's backstory.** The VSL says Zane "never sold anything in his life". His own testimonial says he had a sales job at $1,500/month. Until the VSL changes, don't run ads saying "Zane came from sales". Viewers would hear the opposite seconds later.
3. **Where the companies are.** The VSL says US, UK and Canada. An ad that says "Dubai contacts" won't match it. Confirm with Mike first (see open questions).
4. **Placement wording.** Match the VSL's own words: "I will train you and I will place you", "I have direct relationships with companies who are hiring right now", "If a role doesn't work out, I find you another one". Never say "guarantee"; the VSL doesn't.
5. **Coaching.** "I do call reviews. I help you improve" supports "Mike coaches you himself". But the first call is "one of my team", so ads shouldn't promise that Mike calls you.
6. **Training length.** Say 90 days, not "two weeks" (#12 is out of line).
7. **CTA.** "Short application → a call from the team within 12 hours." Keep "a minute" / "short form". #8's "two minutes" is a small mismatch.
8. **Income framing.** The VSL opens on "$2,500 a month or more… the ceiling is there and it's not moving". That fits capped-commission salespeople, so it's a good bridge for ad hooks.
9. **Proof gap.** The VSL has no student from a sales job. For this audience, the strongest real stories are **Jason** (insurance broker, Oct 2026) and **James** (appointment setter). Neither appears in the VSL, so ads using them are fine but won't be "paid off" on the page.
10. **Origin story.** Pick one. The VSL story is the course-and-Loom one. The "packed train / same desk" story in older ads contradicts it, and the VSL itself says both "two years" (05:29) and "three years" (09:29).
