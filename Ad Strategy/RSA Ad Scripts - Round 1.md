# RSA Ad Scripts — Round 1

**For:** people in 9-5 sales jobs (car sales, corporate, insurance and finance, setters and SDRs) who want to move into remote sales for the freedom.
**What we sell:** placement, not a course. Mike gets you ready, then puts you straight in front of companies he already works with. If a role doesn't work out, he puts you in the next one.
**Format:** Mike to camera, 45–70 seconds each.
**Style:** School of Yalla. Call out the moment they've lived, say what it costs them, show how placement gets them out, then a CTA that shows them they could be working in the next week.

---

## Script 1 — Not a Course

**On-screen text (first 3 seconds):** Paid for the course. Still no role.

> If you've already paid for a closing course and you still don't have a role, this is for you.
>
> Listen. It's not your skill. The problem is getting in front of the companies that are actually hiring.
>
> You did the training. You've sent the Looms, filled in the applications, messaged the offer owners. But every role gets posted in the same few places, hundreds of people apply, and you're one name in the pile. So you hear nothing back, and you start wondering if you wasted the money.
>
> I know, because I've been there. I drained my savings on a course, then spent two years sending Looms to fifty companies a day before anyone hired me. Now I work directly with the companies that hire closers, not just to place myself, but to place you too. Zane came to me after another mentor never placed him. I placed him, and he's now making nine thousand dollars a month.
>
> Another course won't fix this. What gets you hired is someone who puts you straight in front of those companies. That's what I do. And if a role doesn't work out, I put you in the next one.
>
> If you can sell and you're willing to be coached, you could be on your first calls by next week. Click below and I'll show you exactly how.

**Alt hooks**
- "I'm not going to sell you a sales course. I'm going to tell you why most of them don't get you hired."
- "If you're in sales and thinking about going remote, don't buy another course."

**Recording notes:** This is the main ad, so film it first. Use a plain room with no props. Say "Access was." slowly and hold it for a beat. B-roll on the Zane line: a 3-second clip from his testimonial.

## Script 2 — The Cap

> You hit your number. Again. And your pay looks exactly like it did last month.
>
> That's what a capped commission plan does. Close ten or close twenty, and past a certain point the company keeps the difference.
>
> And the only way up is a promotion that depends on who you know, or a pay rise that barely moves.
>
> You already do the hard part. Building rapport, handling objections, getting someone to a decision. That skill works anywhere.
>
> In remote high-ticket sales, you close on Zoom for companies selling programmes worth thousands, and you're paid on what you close.
>
> I get you ready, then put you straight in front of companies I already work with. If a role doesn't work out, I put you in the next one.
>
> If you're good at your job and tired of being capped, click below and see exactly how you could be closing remotely within the next week.

**Alt hooks**
- "Hit target. Same pay as last month."
- "If your commission is capped no matter how well you sell, this is for you."

**Recording notes:** Deliver it calm and a bit dry. The viewer should think "that's literally my job". Don't play it angry. Optional B-roll: a blurred commission-plan PDF or a payslip.

**Recording notes:** Film at night with warm lamp light to match the Sunday-night mood. Speed up a little on the list of four pains, then pause before "Here's what nobody…". B-roll: one second from each of the three testimonials during the proof line.

## Recording notes (all scripts)

- **Setup:** phone vertical (9:16) at eye level, window light on Mike's face, quiet room, clip-on mic. Keep the head and shoulders inside a 4:5 crop too.
- **First 3 seconds:** start on the hook, straight to the lens. No "hey guys" and no intro.
- **Don't read it word for word.** Learn the beats and say it naturally. Keep these exact: the numbers, "If a role doesn't work out, I put you in the next one", and the CTA.
- **Hooks:** film each script's 3 hooks back to back, then the body once. The editor joins them, so we get 3 ads per script.
- **Tone:** calm and direct, like talking to one person. Pause after the hook and after the pain line.
- **Captions:** burned in, big, one line at a time.
- **Never say:** guarantee, guaranteed job, get rich, passive income, uncapped, or anything about the viewer's money ("broke", "can't pay rent").
