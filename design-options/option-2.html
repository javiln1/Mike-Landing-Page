Remote Sales Academy — Field Notes

The sales career you built in an office can travel further than you think.

Mike Akerele explains how working salespeople are moving into remote high-ticket roles without discarding the experience they already earned.

Mike on the difference between being good at sales and building a career that is not tied to one location.

I. The setup

You are not starting again. You are repositioning.

Most working salespeople already have the hardest part: they can speak to customers, handle pressure, and keep a conversation moving.

Remote high-ticket sales demands a more deliberate process, but it does not require you to throw away the career you have already built.

II. The evidence

The change becomes credible when you see who has already made it.

James — Appointment setter
“I went from earning $2,000 a month to $6,700 in my first month after placement.”
Sam — Former bricklayer
“I left inconsistent site work and now earn $8,000 a month working remotely.”

III. The next step

The application is a short conversation about where you are now.

Answer a few direct questions. If the Academy fits your experience, goals, and timing, you will be invited to choose a time with the team.
